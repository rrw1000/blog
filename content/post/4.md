One of this year's admissions questions at Cambridge was about L-systems, and to check that the way I thought the example should work, was how it actually worked, I wrote a quick L-system renderer in [threejs](https://threejs.org/). An installation of this is now available at [https://rrw1000.github.io/lrender/](https://rrw1000.github.io/lrender/) and you can get the (fairly horrid!) source code at [https://github.com/rrw1000/lrender](https://github.com/rrw1000/lrender).
